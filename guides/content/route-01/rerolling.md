Rerolling allows you to save months of hunting while boosting your early game progress as well as securing something useful for later as well.

## What, Why, Who

Rerolling is starting a fresh account, playing through the tutorial, checking your catches, and doing it again until you get something good.
 
It can take months before you find a permanent Pokémon through normal gameplay. Starting out, many days you won't even encounter 6 Pokémon and there will be no guarantee that any of the ones you do encounter will be viable species let alone good catches. By rerolling, you can see 6 Pokémon ever ~10 minutes, with 2 guaranteed to be viable species (Pikachu and Kanto starter). Starting the game with a permanent Pokémon can save you months later and boost your progress early on.

If you either are just starting or already have an account but no permanent Pokémon and don't mind starting over, then rerolling may be worth considering.

## How to Reroll

### Saving Accounts

**If you want to save an account**, whether it be the one you already had or one you came across while rerolling (even if it may not be up to standards), do **NOT** follow step 10. Instead, note down the Support ID and Transfer Code, then either "Clear Data" from your phone's settings for the app or uninstall and reinstall the app.
* **Support ID:** Main Menu > Profile Icon > Support ID (Tap to display)
* **Transfer Code:** Main Menu > Settings and More > Link Accounts > Check Transfer Code

### Rerolling Instructions

1. Complete the initial setup steps (language, country, date of birth, etc.).
   * You do not need to use your real birthday as there are currently no birthday goodies, but you will not be able to change it later.
   * Do not make yourself underage (unless you actually are). Child accounts cannot add friends and require more hours of sleep per night. Guardian approval (through an online process) is needed to change an account from a child account to a normal one.
2. Go through the initial tutorial explanation. Tapping the screen will skip through the slow dialogue.
   * You should read it the first time for learning purposes.
3. Do not check Pikachu's stats, give it a nickname, or set your bedtime yet. You can do it all later.
4. At the sleep tutorial ("Let's Get Sleeping"), choose Skip (wording may be different on your device).
   * If you accidentally press Start Sleep Tracking, you can End Session immediately to return to this screen.
5. Select either Snoozing or Slumbering Sleep Type.
   * Explained in the following section.
6. Complete the catching tutorial and tap through the Sleep Session Results.
7. At the catch review ("A New Helper"), press Skip in the top right.
8. Complete the rest of the tutorial.
9. Check your Pokémon.
10. If there is nothing good, Menu > General Settings > Other > Delete My Account. Close the app and start over.

You can check the Pokémon as you go and reset at step 7, but it is easier to screenshot Pokémon at step 9 and resetting in step 10 saves you from two separate large downloads (~500 MB after step 1 and ~200 MB at step 8) that you would otherwise have to do each time.

As is explained in [Early Game Progression Guide](./early-progression), Berry Specialists and Skill Specialists with Strength-producing main skills (Charge Strength S and M in this case) are very important for early game and, as a result, are our goal for rerolling. Pikachu is a Berry Specialist that you are guaranteed to receive on each reroll, but we should still focus on maximizing our chances of seeing more options.

### Snoozing

Out of 8 possible Pokémon, there is/are:
* 2 good Strength Skill Specialists - :eevee: Eevee (as Espeon) and :mareep: Mareep
* 1 good Support Skill Specialist - :eevee: Eevee (as Vaporeon)

Despite there being only 2 good options in the entire pool, it seems that the likelihood of seeing at least one of these each reroll is actually quite high. Additionally, Strength Skill Specialists have the most flexibility when it comes to acceptable builds and provide a stronger start than Berry Specialists.

However, Strength Skill Specialists require 3-4 of an item called Main Skill Seed. Without getting too much into the details, Main Skill Seeds are not a limited resource but they are a rather scarce one. Using Main Skill Seeds on a good Strength Skill Specialist at the start of the game is a great investment but it will delay some of your later important uses for them by a few months, especially if you aren't planning on spending money on the game.

### Slumbering

Out of 13 possible Pokemon, there is/are:
* 3 good Berry Specialists: :doduo: Doduo, :pichu: Pichu, :spheal: Spheal
* 2 good Ingredient Specialists: :geodude: Geodude and :larvitar: Larvitar
* 1 good Support Skill Specialist: :magnemite: Magnemite

The likelihood of seeing a good Berry Specialist in Slumbering appears to be lower than the likelihood of seeing a good Strength Skill Specialist in Snoozing. Additionally, good Berry Specialists will not provide as strong of a start as good Strength Skill Specialists.

However, the rate of appearance is still good and there is no Main Skill Seed considerations as Berry Specialists do not need them. There are also good Ingredient Specialists, which can be a factor in committing to an account.

### Dozing

Out of 9 possible Pokémon, there is/are:
* 1 good Strength Skill Specialist: :psyduck: Psyduck
* 2 good Ingredient Specialists: :bellsprout: Bellsprout and :croagunk: Croagunk
* 1 good Support Skill Specialist: :gulpin: Gulpin

Though Psyduck is a strong Strength Skill Specialist, it requires checking the game much more frequently than normal for it to perform at a satisfactory level. For this reason, Psyduck is not usually recommended and, as a result, there are no good Strength Skill Specialists in the pool so Dozing should not be chosen.

### Recommendation

Snoozing options will provide the strongest start, but you'll have to pay more attention to your early game Main Skill Seed usage. Slumbering will still provide a strong start, just not as strong as Snoozing, and they also don't need Main Skill Seeds.

Both are good options, you can even do a mix if you want.

## Checking Your Pokémon

This section will provide an overview of what you want when rerolling, but there is some nuance that is not covered here and it is better to let experienced players check your Pokémon for you. It is recommended that you join the [r/PokémonSleep Discord server](https://discord.gg/S836eMxgzY) and send your Pokémon in **#route-01-questions-and-answers** to have them checked. Screenshot them according to the pinned guide in that channel to ensure all important information is included. Be sure to mention that you are rerolling and the standards given below are what you need. Ideally wait for someone with a **Professor Neroli's Aide** :nerolisaide: role to check for you.

### Reroll Standards

Goal is **one S tier** OR **2 A tiers including at least one Berry or Strength Skill Specialist**, only considering the good Pokémon mentioned earlier. If you have gotten tired of rerolling or aren't interested in grinding that hard to begin with, you can opt for **one A tier Berry or Strength Skill Specialist and one B tier**. Even if you don't want to settle for this, you should save these accounts just in case.

|**S tier**||
|:-|:-|
|Berry Specialist|BFS + HB<br>BFS + HSM + HSS/+SoH|
|Strength Skill Specialist|BFS + HB<br>BFS + HSM/STM + any speed/trigger|

|**A tier**||
|:-|:-|
|Berry Specialist|BFS + any speed|
|Strength Skill Specialist|BFS + any speed/trigger|
|Ingredient Specialist|Three of the same ingredient (AAA) with:<br>-  IFM + IFS/+IF/HSM/HB<br>- HSM + two out of IFS/+IF/HB|

|**B tier**|Specific stats are not given|
|:-|:-|
|Ingredient Specialist|Same first and second ingredient (AAx) with good stats for temporary use<br>Same second and third ingredient (ABB) with good stats for permanent use|
|Support Skill Specialist|Good stats for permanent use|


>[!IMPORTANT]
> You may find different resources telling you what standards you should set for your permanent Pokémon. Those are made for normal gameplay where you see ~8 Pokémon each day. Rerolling gives you 6 Pokémon every ~10 minutes, so you should be much pickier.
>
> In the same vein, these standards should not be applied to anything except rerolling. You will hinder your progress if you attempt to use these standards for normal gameplay.

## After You Finish Rerolling
	
If you joined the Discord server, ask in **#route-01-questions-and-answers** for help doing the following. Ideally wait for someone with a **Professor Neroli's Aide** :nerolisaide: role to answer.
1. Manually input a sleep session for the day before to get an extra Bonus Biscuit for your first sleep session.
   * If you plan on getting Premium Pass, do that first. Then, before manually inputting a sleep session for the day before, input one for two days ago, if you can do so, so that you get an extra 100 Sleep Points.
2. If there is a global DP event going on currently and the global DP from yesterday and today is enough to get a guaranteed 5* spawn, manually input a 1.5 hr sleep session for earlier in the day today.
3. Ask how best to use your Master Biscuit (from the forced tutorial) and your two Bonus Biscuits after your first regular sleep session.
   * You should ask for guidance on what to catch for the first week or so anyway.
4. Fill your friend list. You can use **#indigo-league-sleep-sprint** (only if you are willing to use a candy-sending tier list), **#route-01-friend-codes**, and **#friend-codes**. Consider signing up for [Pleep Matchmaker](https://pleep-matchmaker.vercel.app/) as well.
