All players receive a free 14-day no-cost trial for Premium Pass. With proper planning, you can maximize the value received from this trial.

## What is Premium Pass?

Premium Pass is a monthly subscription service.

Its benefits include:
* Upgrading your daily Bonus Biscuit (3 pip) to a Premium Bonus Biscuit (4 pip)
* Additional 100 Sleep Points after your first sleep session each day
* Monthly gift of 1,000 Sleep Points and a Good-Camp Ticket
* Access to the Premium Exchange alongside the Regular Exchange
  * Contains some items not available in the Regular Exchange (notably Sub Skill Seed and Great Biscuits) and others that are discounted (notably Main Skill Seed and Poké Biscuits)
* Full Sleep Data records instead of only the last 30 days
* A diary to write notes

After 3 uninterrupted months of active Premium Pass, Handy Candy L x1, Sub Skill Seed x1, and Dream Cluster M x1 are given as Loyalty Rewards.

All players get a 14-day no-cost trial for Premium Pass. During this trial, you receive all the benefits, including the monthly gift, but the trial will not count towards your Loyalty Rewards.

> [!NOTE]
> Your Premium Pass trial must be used before you can subscribe to Premium Pass.

## Double Dipping

No matter when you use your trial, the benefits you receive will be the same. However, because the Premium Exchange restocks at the end of the month (just like the Regular Exchange), if you save up Sleep Points and start your trial just before the end of the month, you can buy out the stock of the Premium Exchange twice. This is referred to as **double dipping**.

> [!TIP]
> If you plan on subscribing to Premium Pass for 1 month, the same principles can be applied to your timing and savings.

The items to save up for are:
* Poké Biscuit x10 (100 x 10 = 1,000 Sleep Points)
* Great Biscuit x5 (400 x 5 = 2,000 Sleep Points)
* Sub Skill Seed (1,400 Sleep Points)
* Main Skill Seed (1,800 Sleep Points)
* (Optional) Handy Candy M x3 (500 x 3 = 1,500 Sleep Points)

This is 6,200 (+1,500 optional) Sleep Points total for one stock, meaning you need **12,400 (+3,000 optional) Sleep Points** to buy it twice.

Over the course of your trial, you will get 1,400 Sleep Points through your regular sleep sessions (assuming 100 Sleep Score) and 2,400 additional Sleep Points through Premium Pass benefits. So you need to save **at least 8,600 (+3,000 optional) Sleep Points** before starting your trial.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are factoring your regular sleep session Sleep Points into your double dip spending, make sure you can still buy the necessary items from the Regular Exchange.

### Premium Bonus Biscuit

Premium Bonus Biscuit makes it much easier to catch Pokémon as you don't run the risk of 5 pip Pokémon getting full before you catch them. Timing your double dip so that it at least partially overlaps with an event that has a boosted spawn you wish to hunt can squeeze a bit more value out of your trial. This is a much smaller improvement and should only be considered if it doesn't significantly delay your double dip.
