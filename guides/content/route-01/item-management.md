## :pokebiscuit: Biscuits

### Biscuit Economy

The number of Biscuits available to purchase with Sleep Points each month differs depending on whether a player is F2P or Premium Pass:
- **All** players have access to **30 Poké Biscuits** for *150 Sleep Points* each from the Regular Exchange.
- **Premium Pass** players also have access to **10 Poké Biscuits** for *100 Sleep Points* each and **5 Great Biscuits** for *400 Sleep Points* each from the Premium Exchange.

There’s a 27% chance to receive at least one Biscuit from your free Daily Gift in the General Exchange.

### Typical Catch Frequency

* **F2P** players can expect to catch Pokémon **every other day** on average, using their daily 3 pip Bonus Biscuit and purchased Biscuits.
* **Premium Pass** players can expect to catch Pokémon **every day** on average, using their daily 4 pip Premium Biscuit and purchased Biscuits.

Pay attention to your Biscuit expenditure! Consider any upcoming events with increased spawn rates that may need Biscuits allocated. Spending too many Biscuits at the start of the month can make it difficult to manage Sleep Points later in the month.

If a player logs two sleep sessions in a day, the daily Bonus/Premium Biscuit **must be used in the first session** and will not be available in the second session.

### Notable Biscuit Mechanics

There are two baseline mechanics to be aware of:
* After feeding a Pokémon its first Biscuit, there is a 50% chance of it becoming "All Full" with each subsequent Biscuit fed. "All Full" Pokémon cannot be fed more Biscuits during that sleep session.
* There is an 8% chance for Biscuits to crit, giving 3x the points, and a 2% chance for Biscuits to full crit, instantly catching the Pokémon.
  * Hungry Pokémon have a 90% chance to crit and a 10% chance to full crit.

### General Catching Strategies

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You do not need to catch every Pokémon you feed. You can feed your Bonus/Premium Biscuit and finish feeding the next time that Pokémon appears.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Feed Pokémon because they are good, not because they are hungry.** There is no point catching something you will never use.

**If there is a single Pokémon of interest**, feed it the Bonus/Premium Biscuit first in case it crits.

**If there are multiple Pokémon of interest**, feed one two Poké Biscuits first. Finish it with the Bonus/Premium Biscuit (or a Poké Biscuit if one crit) if is not full. If it is full, then feed the other Pokémon your Bonus/Premium Biscuit.

**If there are multiple of the same Pokémon of interest**, you can catch one and check its stats before catching another by following these steps:
1. Catch the first of that Pokémon (and any other Pokémon you might want to catch).
   * Any biscuits used here are **permanent**, this method will not refund you biscuits.
2. Finish Snack Time.
   * You must use your Bonus/Premium Biscuit before this, otherwise it will be fed to a random Pokémon.
3. Choose your Session Photo and go through the two Sleep Research Results screens.
4. At the "A New Helper" screen, view Stats of your catch.
   * Do not press OK on the last Pokémon you've caught, it will end your Sleep Research.
5. If you are not satisfied with the stats, close the app and open it again. This will take you back to Snack Time.
   * This will **not** reroll the stats of the Pokémon you've already caught, only give you an opportunity to catch more.
6. Repeat as many times as necessary.

Keep your monthly biscuit stock in mind while doing this.

### Feeding Evolved Pokémon

The upside of catching evolved Pokémon is that you **save on candy (and potentially evolution items)** because they are already evolved and are a higher level when caught.

The downsides of feeding evolved Pokémon are:
* **Higher pip cost** makes each catch more costly
* Less frequent catching makes **building Friendship Level (FL) more difficult**
* **Free skill level(s) lost** for each evolution missed

Higher pip cost is a universal downside, though being hungry does offset this and sometimes there aren't any worthwhile base stage Bonus Biscuit targets.

How much the other two downsides matter depends on specialty:
* Berry Specialists:
  * Building FL is a crucial part of the Berry Specialist hunt as Berry Finding S (BFS) is practically essential and FL10 makes it much easier to get. You can progress Base Stage Pokémon's FL much faster as they are cheaper and more common, which also means you can capitalize on that FL more often. Not being able to do so for evolved Pokémon is a significant downside, though you can still get lucky and roll full odds BFS. 
  * Loss of free skill levels is a very small downside. There are some Berry Specialists with useful skills and a lower skill level hurts their usefulness, but that is a minor inconvenience.
* Ingredient Specialists:
  * Building FL is not part of the Ingredient Specialists hunt. In fact, while FL10 makes a usable Helping Bonus (HB) roll more likely, it makes usable rolls overall less likely as there is one fewer slot available for Ingredient Finder sub skills. Because of this, building FL can instead be a considered a downside if you don't think the increased usable HB roll likelihood is a worthwhile trade-off.
  * Loss of free skill levels is a very small downside. There are some Ingredient Specialists with useful skills and a lower skill level hurts their usefulness, but that is a minor inconvenience.
* Skill Specialists:
  * Building FL on:
    * Support Skill Specialists is nice for HB, but not the largest issue if you don't as it isn't essential on most roles.
    * Strength-producing Skill Specialists is just as important as it is on Berry Specialists, making it a significant downside if you don't.
  * Loss of free skill levels is a massive downside as Main Skill Seeds (MSS) are costly and scarce. There are very few Skill Specialists that are temporarily worth using without maxed main skill and none that are permanently worth using.

Taking all of that into account, these are the general guidelines when it comes to feeding evolved Pokémon:
1. **Never feed evolved Skill Specialists.** No amount of candies and evolutions items saved makes up for the extra MSS cost.
2. **Only feed evolved Berry Specialists your Bonus Biscuit when there are no other options.** You'll be praying for full odds BFS, maybe you'll get lucky.
2. **Consider feeding evolved Ingredient Specialists when there are no higher priority options**, usually just your Bonus Biscuit. It can help you delay/avoid FL10-related issues.
When you do feed evolved Pokémon, it should mainly be when they are hungry, but you can feed non-hungry ones if one biscuit would finish them off.

### Great and Ultra Biscuit Usage

Great Biscuit usage depends on whether a player is F2P or Premium Pass. Premium Pass players can purchase 5 a month while F2P has a more limited supply through Research Rank rewards, Event Shops during Legendary Research events and New Years, and bundles.
* **F2P** players should save them for hungry 16 pip Pokémon, but can use them more freely once their catch priorities narrow.
* **Premium Pass** players can use them more freely, even on 5 pip Pokémon after using 2 Poké Biscuits on them first.

Ultra Biscuits are scarce for all players, with only a few available through Achievements and Sleep Style Goals and the main source being bundles.
* **All** players should save them for hungry important 16+ pip Pokémon, including Legendary and Mythical Pokémon if those are currently being chased or prioritized.

### Master Biscuit

Master Biscuits cost 4,000 Sleep Points, which is a *significant* portion of your monthly income. They are *inefficient* when it comes to Sleep Points spent per catch, regardless of the Pokémon's required pips, but have the advantage of not needing multiple encounters. For the vast majority of players, buying them is **highly discouraged**.

If you wish to buy Master Biscuits, it is recommended that you:
1. Limit your Poké Biscuit purchases each month, otherwise you will limit your Main Skill Seed purchasing frequency
2. Only use them to catch Legendary Pokémon as they have the highest pips (30) and least frequently spawn
   * Limit yourself to catching one or two Legendary Pokémon species, do not try to catch every Legendary that you see

### Catching Shiny Pokémon

Shinies are **GUARANTEED** to full crit on the first Biscuit they are fed. **Only feed them a SINGLE Poké Biscuit.**

## :mainseed: Main and Sub Skill Seeds

### How Seeds Work

Main Skill Seeds (MSS) increase the main skill level of a Pokémon by 1. Each Main Skill has a maximum level, MSS will not increase the level beyond this.

Sub Skill Seeds (SSS) upgrade a random unlocked eligible sub skill. Eligible sub skills are ones with an existing immediate upgrade (S→M and M→L) that is not already in another sub skill slot. Currently, the only S sub skill *without* an upgrade is Berry Finding S and the only M sub skill *with* an upgrade is Inventory Up M.

### Seed Economy

MSS and SSS availability differs based on whether a players is F2P or Premium Pass.
* **All** players can purchase one MSS for 3,600 Sleep Points in the **Regular Exchange** each month, though it is more likely that you will buy it every other month.
* **Premium Pass** players can purchase one MSS for 1,800 Sleep Points and one SSS for 1,400 Sleep Points in **Premium Exchange** each month. It is recommended to buy these every month. They also receive one free SSS every 3 months through Premium Pass Loyalty Rewards.

Other sources for seeds:
* Through **Achievements and Sleep Style Goals**, players get 10 MSS (with 6 being late game) and 11 SSS (with 8 being late game).
* **Event Shops** during Legendary Research events and New Years will have one MSS purchasable with event currency. These usually occur at least 3 times each year.
* **Developer Gifts** usually provide at least three MSS each year, two during Anniversary Fest and one during Holiday.
* **Event Bundles** purchasable with diamonds.

SSS are an extremely limited resource for F2P players! Bundles are the only real source of SSS for F2P players outside of game progression's limited supply.

### MSS Strategies

Skill Specialists need 3-7 MSS to max their Main Skill level, keeping in mind that evolution increases Main Skill level by one. Do **NOT** use MSS on non-Skill Specialists in the early game.

> [!IMPORTANT] Further reading
> Check out [Meta Skill Specialists](../pokemon-evaluation/meta-skill-specialists) to learn about Skill Specialist priorities.

### SSS Strategies

F2P players should only seed **permanent** Pokémon with a **strong** preference for the following order of priority:
1. Ingredient specialists: Ingredient Finding S → M
2. Skill specialists: Skill Trigger S → M
3. Berry specialists: Helping Speed S → M
4. Ingredient/Skill specialists: Helping Speed S → M

Premium Pass players can afford to seed temporary Pokémon if the output improvement is worthwhile.

## :neutralmint: Neutralizing Mints

Neutralizing Mints neutralize the positive and negative effects of a Pokémon's nature. These are an **extremely** limited resource with **only 1 given per year** during Anniversary Fest. Players receive one additional Mint upon clearing the Mint tutorial for the first time. You can only have two Mints in your bag at one time.

### Mint Usage

Only **useful** Pokémon with otherwise good stats should be considered for Mint usage. Do not Mint something that has no meta-relevant use or something that still would not be a forever Pokémon after neutralizing its nature.

1. **Ingredient Specialists with Ingredient-** and **Skill Specialists with Skill-** are the best candidates for Mint usage as these natures have a significant negative impact on the performance of the Pokémon.
   * Adamant (Speed+ Ingredient-) is still a negative nature for Ingredient Specialists.
   * Naughty (Speed+ Skill-) is:
      * still a negative nature on Support Skill Specialists (anything that cares about procs and nothing else)
      * a slightly negative nature on trigger-build non-Legendary Strength-producing Skill Specialists
      * basically a neutral nature on trigger-build Legendary Skill Specialists
      * a slightly positive nature on BFS-build Strength-producing Skill Specialists
2. **Legendary Pokémon with EXP-** can be considered due to the already high EXP cost of raising them being made even worse by their nature. However, the net impact on of Minting this nature is only 576 Pokémon Candy and ~180k Dream Shards saved.
3. **Speed-** is the lowest priority nature to consider. The impact of this nature on production is only -7%, which is not too significant. Minting this nature should only be considered when doing so would allow a Pokémon to reach specific performance breakpoints or if a Mint needs to be used due to bag limit.
   * Modest (Ingredient+ Speed-) is a positive nature for Ingredient Specialists.
   * Calm (Skill+ Speed-) is a positive nature for all Skill Specialists with trigger builds, but a negative nature for Strength-producing Skill Specialists with BFS builds.

The following natures are, at worst, minor inconveniences with negligible impacts on performance and should not be Minted:
* **Energy-**
* **Non-Legendary Pokémon with EXP-**
* **Non-Skill Specialists with Skill-**
* **Non-Ingredient Specialists with Ingredient+**

## :handyS: Handy Candies

**Handy Candy S** can be used as needed or if you need to free up bag space. It is not rare and does not need to be hoarded.

Consider saving **Handy Candy M and L** to use on species whose candy are uncommon or rare (e.g. Legendaries, Mythicals, 16 pips, Pseudo-Legendaries, etc.).

## :typeS: Type Candies

**Type Candy S** can be used as needed or if you need to free up bag space. It is not rare once you start leaving Pokémon on Munchlax's Nap Island and does not need to be hoarded.

Consider saving **Type Candy M and L** to use on species whose candy are uncommon or rare (e.g. Legendaries, Mythicals, 16 pips, Pseudo-Legendaries, etc.).

## :ezzz: E-Zzz Travel Tickets

E-Zzz Travel Ticket allows you to change sites immediately rather than at the start of each week. It can be used to go to the same map you are currently on but with a new Snorlax.

This will reset:
* Snorlax's strength to 0
  * During event weeks that start with a fixed Snorlax strength (referred to as "big Snorlax"), strength will reset to that value instead
* Snorlax's preferred meal type
* Snorlax's non-fixed favorite berries
*  Uncompleted Snorlax rating-based Weekly Missions
   * Completed but unclaimed missions will not be reset

The two main uses for these tickets are:
1. Changing sites at the end of the week to deposit your weekly Area Bonus on a different map, especially when you still have your Novice Researcher Bonus
2. Rerolling meal type and/or favorite berries to something more favorable, usually during events

One E-Zzz Travel Ticket can be purchased for *100 Sleep Points* from the Regular Exchange each month. All players are recommended to purchase it every month as it is cheap and you will likely need multiple when the time comes to use it.

> [!IMPORTANT] Further Reading
> Check out [Area Bonus/Expert Bonus](../introductory-concepts/area-bonus-expert-bonus) to learn more about E-Zzz Travel Ticket usage.

## :expert: Expert Tickets

Expert Tickets are required to go to Expert Mode maps. If you wish to use E-Zzz Travel Ticket to go to an Expert Mode map, you must also use an Expert Ticket each time.

All players receive one free Expert Ticket upon unlocking their first Expert Mode map.

Three Expert Tickets can be purchased for *200 Sleep Points* from the Regular Exchange each month. One Expert Ticket can be purchased for *100 Sleep Points* from the Premium Exchange each month. However, you can only have two Expert Tickets in your bag at one time.

Unlike E-Zzz Travel Tickets, there is no need to purchase Expert Tickets monthly until you actually start going to Expert Mode maps.

## :camp: Good Camp Tickets

For 7 days (168 hours), Good Camp Ticket (GCT) provides these benefits:
* 50% pot size increase, rounded up
* 20% production boost to all of your Pokémon
* 20% Carry Limit increase to all of your Pokémon
* Bonus spawn that is guaranteed to be hungry (only during the first sleep session of each day)

### GCT Economy

The number of GCT available depends on whether a player is F2P or Premium:
* **All** players receive one free GCT after studying 190 Sleep Styles.
* **Premium Pass** players receive one free GCT every month.

Other sources for GCT:
* **Developer Gifts** may provide one annually.
* **Event Bundles** purchasable with diamonds.

GCT are an extremely limited resource for F2P players! Bundles are the only real source of GCT for F2P players, though SSS bundles are considered higher priorities.

### GCT Usage

**F2P** players should save GCT for Master 20 (M20) attempts on later maps (Lapis Lakeside onwards) and Expert Mode maps. While it is possible to M20 these maps with event boosts only, it can still difficult without a GCT.

**Premium Pass** players can use GCT for:
* M20 attempts on any map
* Events with event currency (Legendary Research, New Years) as more spawns and higher rarity sleep styles give more currency
* New (good) Pokémon release events
* Events with boosted spawn that they wish to hunt
Keep 2-3 GCT in stock for multi-week events (Legendary Research, Anniversary Fest, new map debut, New Years).

> [!TIP]
> Use GCT on the Sunday night before the week you want it active. Sleep at an earlier time the following Sunday, just before your GCT timer runs out. This will get you 8 nights with an extra spawn instead of 7.

## :pillow: Energy Pillows

Energy Pillows should be saved used when your healer is having a bad day during M20 attempts or events where you aim for high strength. You receive a limited number of these through game progression.

## :berryjuice: Berry Juices

Berry Juice is a reduced Energy Pillow (20 energy recovered instead of 50) that is currently only obtainable through Shuckle's main skill. You can only have five Berry Juice in your bag at one time.

Because it is farmable and has a bag limit, you do not need to save Berry Juice and can use it as needed. Before event weeks, you can stock up on five in case your healer has a bad day.

## :whistle: Helper Whistles

Helper Whistles get you three hours worth of helps, a mix of berries and ingredients, but do not trigger main skill. Save these for use during M20 attempts when you are just short of reaching M20 and have enough Whistles to get there. You receive a limited number of these through game progression.

## :clusterS: Dream Shard Clusters

Dream Shard Clusters give more Dream Shards the higher Research Rank (RR) you are.

**Dream Shard Cluster S** can be used as needed or to free up bag space. The amount of Dream Shards obtained at max RR is not always worth the bag space needed to accommodate the clusters until then.

**Dream Shard Cluster M and L** should be saved until max RR, they give 6x and 25x as many Dream Shards as Dream Shard Cluster S, respectively.

## :ingticketS: Ingredient Tickets

Ingredient Tickets can be used as needed or if you need to free up bag space. It is not rare and does not need to be hoarded. Consider saving **Ingredient Ticket M and L** for use during events to assist with cooking.
