Resources are less abundant than the main series Pokémon games or Pokémon GO, so it is important to spend any accrued currencies **effectively** and **efficiently**!

<SubjectiveInfoBanner />

## :sleeppoint: Sleep Points

Excluding Developer Gifts, which often contain Sleep Points, F2P players earn **5,000 Sleep Points** each 30-day month while Premium Pass players earn **9,000 Sleep Points**.

### Sleep Sessions

* **All** players earn a maximum of 100 Sleep Points through Sleep Research, for a total of **3,000 Sleep Points** each month. The Sleep Points earned correspond to the player's Sleep Score for the session. Should a player log two sessions, the total Sleep Points across both sessions is still capped at 100.
* **Premium Pass** players get 100 additional Sleep Points after their first session each day, for an additional **3,000 Sleep Points** each month.

### Good Sleep Day (GSD)

* GSD is a 3-day event that occurs every month.
* GSD Day 1 and Day 3 give 500 Sleep Points each and Day 2 gives 1,000 Sleep Points, for a total of **2,000 Sleep Points** each month.

### Premium Pass Monthly Gift

**Premium Pass** players also get **1,000 Sleep Points** as a gift once per month.

## Optimal Sleep Point Usage

> [!NOTE]
> All recommendations assume a nightly Sleep Score of 100.

While you may not currently need some of these items, you will in the future. When that time comes, you'll often need to use multiple of them at once. It is better to stock up on them in advance as they have limited availability each month.

### Recommended Order of F2P Priorities

* E-Zzz Travel Ticket (100 Sleep Points)
* Poké Biscuit x20 (150 x 20 = 3,000 Sleep Points)

This will leave you with 1,900 Sleep Points each month, enabling a Main Skill Seed (3,600 Sleep Points) purchase every other month. Purchasing fewer Poké Biscuits will allow for more frequent Main Skill Seed purchases.

### Recommended Order of Premium Pass Priorities

* E-Zzz Ticket (100 Sleep Points, Regular Exchange)
* Sub Skill Seed (1,400 Sleep Points, Premium Exchange)
* Main Skill Seed (1,800 Sleep Points, Premium Exchange)
* Great Biscuit x5 (400 x 5 = 2,000 Sleep Points, Premium Exchange)
* Poké Biscuit x10 (100 x 10 = 1,000 Sleep Points, Premium Exchange)

This will leave you with 2,700 Sleep Points each month. Some options you can consider:
* Poké Biscuits (150 Sleep Points, Regular Exchange)
* Handy Candy M (500 Sleep Points, Premium Exchange)
* Main Skill Seed (3,600 Sleep Points, Regular Exchange)
  * Purchasing up to 6 Biscuits from the Regular Exchange each month allows for a Regular Exchange Main Skill Seed purchase every other month.
  * Purchasing an additional 2 Biscuits each month pushes this to every third month, then every fourth month and then every sixth month.

### What about Master Biscuits?

Master Biscuits cost 4,000 Sleep Points, which is a *significant* portion of your monthly income. They are *inefficient* when it comes to Sleep Points spent per catch, regardless of the Pokémon's required pips, but have the advantage of not needing multiple encounters. For the vast majority of players, buying them is **highly discouraged**.

If you wish to buy Master Biscuits, it is recommended that you:
1. Limit your Poké Biscuit purchases each month, otherwise you will limit your Main Skill Seed purchasing frequency
2. Only use them to catch Legendary Pokémon as they have the highest pips (30) and least frequently spawn
   * Limit yourself to catching one or two Legendary Pokémon species, do not try to catch every Legendary that you see

## :diamond: Diamonds

Excluding Achievements, Recipe and Ingredient unlocks, and Sleep Style Goals, you can expect to get roughly **6,500 to 7,050 free Diamonds** each year.

### Daily Gifts

There’s a 35.5% chance to receive Diamonds from your free Daily Gift in the General Exchange, averaging out to 6.675 Diamonds per gift, which is **2,436.375 Diamonds** in 365 days.

### Stamp Cards
* Day 3 gives 10 Diamonds and Day 6 gives 25 Diamonds.
* Fully completing the card gets you 1,820 Diamonds across 52 cards (364 days) each year.
* If you reset the card every 6 days, you get **2,100 Diamonds** across 60 cards (360 days) each year.
  * Reset the card by changing your bedtime. You can change it back immediately after.
  * Dream Shard Cluster S is worth very little so losing Day 7's Cluster is worth the Diamonds gained.

### Weekly Missions

* Two missions give 15 Diamonds each.
  * "Bedtime X times" - Easy mission to complete each week.
  * "Use X candies" - Difficult to complete each week, especially at higher Research Ranks where 200+ candies are needed. Not only is it difficult to spend that many candies almost every week, it is often not the optimal way to use candies.
* Rolling 5 out of the 7 non-guaranteed missions gives 21 possible mission lineups.
  * Completing only "Bedtime X times" (15/21 lineups) averages to 10.714 Diamonds per week, which is **557.128 Diamonds** in 52 weeks.
  * Completing both "Bedtime X times" and "Use X candies" (10/21 have only one mission and 10/21 have both) averages to 21.429 Diamonds per week, which is **1,114.308 Diamonds** in 52 weeks.

### Developer Gifts

Each year, Pokémon Day Broadcast Commemorative Gift gives **1,000 Diamonds** and Anniversary Fest Dream Gifts give **400 Diamonds**.

## Optimal Diamond Usage

### Recommended Order of Priority

Raising your Ingredient Bag capacity to the maximum is the first priority. Though it may not seem useful when you start out, it becomes crucial as your cooking progresses.

Good bundles are the next priority for your diamonds. For F2P, those would be bundles with Main Skill Seeds (MSS), Sub Skill Seeds (SSS) and Good Camp Ticket (GCT), with SSS bundles being the most important. If your Ingredient Bag capacity is in a good place for you, it may be worth delaying expanding it further in favor of grabbing a good bundle.

> [!IMPORTANT] Further reading
> Check out [Item Management](./item-management) to learn why these items are priorities.

Throughout this, you can increase your Pokémon Box and Item Bag capacity as needed.
