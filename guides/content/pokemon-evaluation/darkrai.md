Darkrai is a Mythical Pokémon that functions best when built as a Strength Pokémon.

## What stat slots can Darkrai roll?

When you first get Darkrai, it will only have Bad Dreams (Charge Strength M) as its main skill, Sausage as its first ingredient, and Bashful as its nature.

![Base Darkrai](./images/base-darkrai.jpg)

Eureka Seed (Darkrai) allows you to choose either an ingredient slot or a sub skill slot to roll. You will be given the option to switch to a random ingredient out of a pool or a random sub skill in that slot, respectively. Sub skill rarity is ignored, so all sub skills have an equal chance of being rolled. Choosing to stay with your current roll rather than switch to the new one gives you 40 Darkrai candy as compensation.

## Is Darkrai good?

Darkrai has very good Strength production **when built properly**. Depending on your ability to mitigate the energy loss from its skill, it has the potential to be a generalist Strength Pokémon that matches many on-map Berry Specialists. On Snowdrop, where Dark is a favored type, it is the strongest Strength Pokémon.

While it has a good selection of ingredient options, its production is too low to be ran as an Ingredient Specialist. Instead, it can be used for supplemental ingredients, much like a Berry Specialist can, with an ingredient list tailored for your needs.

A common misconception is that you need a Dark team for Darkai. In reality, it is just like any other Strength Skill Specialist except you have to plan your proc collection around your team's energy. Using only Dark Pokémon will limit you to Umbreon, a very weak healer that is not worth running over better healers, and a very small selection of Ingredient Specialists, restricting you ability to make top-tier recipes.

### Early Game Usage

Though Darkrai is at its strongest when fully built, blank Darkrai is also quite strong very early. Players have few if any good Pokémon at this stage of the game, often running very bad ones just to fill their team. Darkrai is a much higher level than other Pokémon when caught and its natural berry strength combined with its main skill make it stronger than most other options a player has.

Players usually do not have a healer at that point, so there is no way to mitigate the energy loss from the procs. For this reason, you should Sneaky Snack Darkrai during the day and only collect procs later in the day when your team has fallen below 10% energy.

> [!IMPORTANT] Further reading
> Check out [Sneaking Snacking](../../introductory-concepts/sneaky-snacking) to learn more about Sneaky Snacking.

Importantly, the catching advice for Darkrai does not change and you still should not sink resources into catching it just for early game usage. When you do get Darkrai, you can consider using it then.

Additionally, it is not worth spending Main Skill Seeds on Darkrai when using it for this purpose. Though it will further increase its usefulness at this time, it will only be beneficial for a brief time (unless you get very lucky with Eureka Seeds as well) and it will significantly delay the higher priority Main Skill Seed targets.

## How should I use Eureka Seeds?

These steps prioritize getting Darkrai online by lv. 50 over making it perfect at lv. 70.
1. Roll each of the first 3 sub skill slots (lv. 10, 25, 50) once.
2. Reroll each of these sub skill slots until you get any of **Berry Finding S**, **Helping Bonus**, **Helping Speed M**, or **Skill Trigger M**.
   * If you roll the **S** version of an **M** sub skill, keep it. A Sub Skill Seed is cheaper than a Eureka Seed (for Premium Pass) and it would likely take multiple Eureka Seeds to roll the upgraded **M** versions in its place. However, do not Sub Skil Seed it until you have finished rolling the other slots as you may roll the **M** version in one of them.
3. Roll each of the empty ingredient slots once.
4. Reroll each ingredient slot until you have a list that works well for you.
5. Roll and reroll the 4th sub skill slot (lv. 70) until you get one of the above sub skills.
If you wish to aim for a perfect build by lv. 70, potentially taking longer to be usable at lv. 50, roll and reroll the 4th sub skill slot at the same time as the first 3 slots. Keep in mind that taking Darkrai to lv. 70 takes over twice as much EXP as it does for normal Pokémon.

> [!NOTE]
> It is generally better to reject rolls that aren't what you need and receive 40 Darkrai candy instead. While it doesn't impact the build process if you don't, rejecting rolls is a great way to accumulate Darkrai candy, which is otherwise rare.
>
> If you are using Darkrai while in the process of building it, certain sub skills can still provide value despite not being one of the desired ones. Notable examples include Sleep EXP Bonus to help you level Darkrai faster if you are leaving it overnight and Skill Level Up S/M to increase your Bad Dreams strength if you haven't maxed the skill level yet.
