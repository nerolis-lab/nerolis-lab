Mythical Pokémon have the benefits of all specialties. You can only catch one of each species, so they have unique mechanics for customizations.

## What are Mythicals?

Mythical Pokémon are the only All Specialists. That means:
* like Berry Specialists, they find 2 berries per berry help at baseline
* like Ingredient Specialists, they find higher numbers of ingredients per ingredient help
* like Skill Specialists, they can store 2 skill procs

There are currently two Mythical Pokémon in the game: Darkrai and Mew.

## When can I see Mythicals?
Mythical Pokémon only appear during their respective events.

* :mew: Mew: Good Sleep Day
* :darkrai: Darkrai: New Moon Day

These are monthly 3-day events where Mythical Pokémon can appear as a bonus spawn during your first sleep session each day. If you don't see a Mythical Pokémon one day, it will be more likely to appear the next. You can expect to see about 1 each per event, but it is possible to see more.

While NMD does also boost shiny rate, Mythical Pokémon cannot be shiny.

## How do Mythicals work?

They cost 25 pips but get 3 free pips each time you see them. Your first catch of each Mythical Pokémon will get you the Pokémon and one Eureka Seed for that Pokémon. Subsequent catches will get you only a Eureka Seed for that Pokémon.

Mythical Pokémon start out with most stats blank. Each Mythical Pokémon will have the same starting stats for all players. For example, Darkrai will have a Bashful Nature and Sausage in its first ingredient slot.

Eureka Seeds allow you alter a single stat of your choosing, with the limitation that stat slots must be filled in the order they appear. When you choose a stat to alter, a new option will be rolled at random, with all potential options being weighted equally. If you were altering a stat that already had a value, you will be given the choice to either accept the new option or keep the current value. Rejecting the new option and staying with your current value will give 40 candy for that Pokémon as compensation.

## Should I catch Mythicals?

<SubjectiveInfoBanner />

Mythical Pokémon take too long to come online and cost too much for them to be worth hunting in early game. Let them accumulate free pips and, when they only have one pip left (every 8th encounter), finish them off with a Poké Biscuit. If you have no other priority catch targets, you can feed them your Bonus Biscuit. As you progress in the game and have fewer catch targets, you can afford to feed them more often.

Treat them like a long-term project for late game rather than something to heavily invest resources in from the start.

### Should I use Master Biscuits?

Master Biscuits are expensive and inefficient when it comes to Sleep Points spent per catch, but have the advantage of not needing multiple encounters. However, if you are at a stage in the game where you are investing resources into catching Mythical Pokémon, this advantage is not of much relevance. Between the free pips from each encounter, your Bonus Biscuit, additional Poké or Great Biscuits, and decreased likelihood to get full, you can catch Mythical Pokémon every second or third encounter. With the expectation of seeing one of each Mythical per month, this makes Master Biscuits a very high cost just to save you one or two months per catch.

For these reasons, it is not recommended to buy or use Master Biscuits on Mythical Pokémon.
