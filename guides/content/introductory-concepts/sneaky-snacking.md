## What is Sneaky Snacking?

When your Pokemon performs a help, it will find either berries or ingredients and put them in its inventory. Every help also has a chance to trigger the Pokemon's main skill. When you tap a Pokemon, it will drop its inventory onto the ground and use any stored skill triggers. Berry Specialists and Ingredient Specialists can store one skill trigger, Skill Specialists can store two.

Each Pokemon's inventory has a certain capacity, called the Carry Limit, and you can see this number on the Pokemon's summary screen. Once your Pokemon has reached its Carry Limit, it stops finding ingredients and checking for skill triggers. Instead, every help is guaranteed to be a berry help that is fed directly to Snorlax. **This is called Sneaky Snacking.**

When your Pokemon Sneaky Snacks, you'll get a pop-up upon opening the game or returning to the home screen telling you how much strength your Snorlax gained this way. This usually happens when you don't check the game for many hours, like overnight, but you can force it to happen by intentionally not emptying a Pokemon's inventory.

## Why is this useful?

For Ingredient Specialists and Skill Specialists, this is **not useful** because it prevents them from doing their job, which is finding ingredients and triggering their skill, respectively.

For Berry Specialists, this **can be useful**. Instead of sometimes finding berries and sometimes finding ingredients, it will only find berries each time. This means it will find more berries in a period of time than it would if it wasn't Sneaky Snacking.

## Should I always be Sneaky Snacking my Berry Specialists?

Not necessarily.

1. **You may want the skill procs.**
   - Strength-producing skills (Charge Strength, Extra Helpful) may produce more strength than you gain through Sneaky Snacking.
   - Cooking skills (Cooking Power-Up, Tasty Chance) can assist in preparation for cooking.
2. **You may want the ingredients it finds.**
   - If you are able to use ingredients for cooking strong recipes, doing so will provide more strength than Sneaky Snacking.
   - Berry strength scales with level so, at lower levels, berries provide less strength than using whatever ingredients your Berry Specialist finds, even if only for filler. The level at which berries become stronger than filler ingredients varies by species.
   - You may want to save up ingredients that are hard for you to get, in order to unlock new dishes or cook stronger meals later.

## Misconception About Inventory Up Subskills and Sneaky Snacking

While it is true that Berry Specialists with Inventory subskills take longer to start Sneaky Snacking, the actual impact of this is negligible for two simple reasons.

1. The majority of helps needed to fill the Berry Specialist's additional inventory will still be berries.
2. Once a Berry Specialist fills its inventory and begins Sneaky Snacking, the additional inventory becomes entirely irrelevant.

As such, Inventory Up subskills should **NOT** be viewed as a negative when evaluating Berry Specialists.
