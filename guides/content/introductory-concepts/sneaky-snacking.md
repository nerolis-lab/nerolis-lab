Sneaky Snacking occurs when your Pokémon's inventory is full. During Sneaky Snacking, every help is guaranteed to be a berry-only help which is fed directly to Snorlax.

## What is Sneaky Snacking?

When your Pokémon performs a help, it will find either berries or ingredients and put them in its inventory. Every help also has a chance to activate the Pokémon's main skill. When you tap a Pokémon, it will drop its inventory onto the ground and use any stored skill procs. Berry Specialists and Ingredient Specialists can store one skill proc, Skill Specialists can store two.

Each Pokémon's inventory has a certain capacity, called the Carry Limit, and you can see this number on the Pokémon's summary screen. Once your Pokémon has reached its Carry Limit, it stops finding ingredients and checking for skill procs. Instead, every help is guaranteed to be a berry help that is fed directly to Snorlax. **This is called Sneaky Snacking.**

When your Pokémon Sneaky Snacks, you'll get a pop-up upon opening the game or returning to the home screen telling you how much strength your Snorlax gained this way. This usually happens when you don't check the game for many hours, like overnight, but you can force it to happen by intentionally not emptying a Pokémon's inventory.

## Why is this useful?

For Ingredient Specialists and Skill Specialists, this is **not useful** because it prevents them from doing their job, which is finding ingredients and activating their skill, respectively.

For Berry Specialists, this **can be useful**. Instead of sometimes finding berries and sometimes finding ingredients, it will only find berries each time. This means it will find more berries in a period of time than it would if it wasn't Sneaky Snacking.

## Should I always be Sneaky Snacking my Berry Specialists?

Not necessarily.

1. **You may want the skill procs.**
   - Strength-producing skills (such as Charge Strength and Extra Helpful) may produce more strength than you gain through Sneaky Snacking.
   - Other skills may be situationally valuable to your team, such as cooking or support skills, to cook stronger meals, maintain energy, or gather resources.
2. **You may want the ingredients it finds.**
   - If you are able to use ingredients for cooking strong recipes, doing so will provide more strength than Sneaky Snacking.
   - Berry strength scales with level. At lower levels, berries provide less strength than using whatever ingredients your Berry Specialist finds, even if only for filler. The level at which berries become stronger than filler ingredients varies by species.
   - You may want to save up ingredients that are hard for you to get, in order to unlock new dishes or cook stronger meals later.
