Area/Expert Bonus rewards time spent on a map by making it easier to gain Strength the next time you go.

## What is Area Bonus

Area Bonus (AB) is a percentage boost applied to all forms of Snorlax Strength generation (berries, cooking, skill).

Each non-Expert Mode map has its own AB percentage that starts at 0%. At the end of each week (4:00 AM on Monday), the map you are currently on gets 5% added to its AB. It does not matter if you spent the entire week on one map and then used E-Zzz Travel Ticket to go to a different map at 3:58 AM on Monday, it will still go to the new map.

### Novice Researcher Bonus (Newbie AB)

Novice Researcher Bonus, commonly referred to as newbie AB, is given to players while their total AB across all maps is less than 250%. Instead of receiving 5% AB at the end of each week, they receive 15%. This is the equivalent of three weeks of AB given in one week.

## What is Expert Bonus

Expert Bonus (EB) is just AB but for Expert Mode (EX) maps. EX maps exist separately from non-EX maps, AB does not overflow to EX maps and EB does not overflow to non-EX maps. Newbie AB does not boost EB and EB does not count towards newbie AB's 250% threshold. Apart from that, EB works exactly the same as AB.

## Area Bonus Limit (AB Cap) and Overflow

There is a limit to how high your AB can be on any map. This limit is often referred to as AB cap.

The limit is 10% at the start of the game and increases by 5% as you reach each Sleep Style Dex Goal.

**If you are not at the limit yet but would gain AB that puts you over the limit**, your AB will reach the limit and any excess will be lost.
* **Example:** Your current map's AB is 40% and your current limit is 50%. If you would receive 15% at the end of the week through newbie AB, you will instead only receive 10%.

**If you are already at the limit on your current map**, your AB you receive will be redistributed to other maps. Starting with Greengrass Isle, each unlocked map that is not at cap will receive 1% AB in sequential order, looping back around until all the AB has been distributed. This is referred to as **AB overflow.**
* **Example:** You are on Cyan Beach with 40% AB, your current limit is 40%, you have Taupe Hollow and Snowdrop Tundra unlocked, and all your unlocked maps apart from Cyan Beach have 30% AB. If you would receive 5% AB at the end of the week, first Greengrass Isle will receive 1%, then Taupe Hollow will receive 1%, then Snowdrop Tundra will receive 1%, then Greengrass Isle will receive 1%, and then Taupe Hollow will receive the final 1%.

## Area Bonus Strategies

### Using E-Zzz Travel Ticket to Move Area Bonus

You can use an E-Zzz Travel Ticket to switch maps any time during the week. Some possible reasons and times to do this:
* If you want to spend your week on one map (for spawns or an event, for example) but put your AB on another map, you can switch maps on Sunday.
   * If you switch Sunday morning, you can get some Snorlax Strength so your Sunday night sleep session on the new map will have better spawns.
   * If you want to put as much Strength into the current map, usually to hit certain rank milestones, then you can switch Sunday night before sleeping, though your sleep session will have low or no Snorlax Strength.
   * You can do your Sunday sleep session on your current map early, either midday or so that you wake up before Monday 4 AM, and then switch maps afterwards. That way you will still have a Sunday sleep session with good Strength and spawns.
   * Go++ users can move their AB to a new map while keeping their Sunday sleep session on their current map.

> [!IMPORTANT] Further reading
> Check out [Pokémon Go++ Secret Techniques]() to learn how to move your AB to another map while keeping your sleep session on the previous map.

* If you are a few sleep styles short of unlocking a new map, you can switch mid-week once you get those styles.
* If a new map is released mid-week, you can switch right when it becomes available.

> [!NOTE]
> Using E-Zzz Travel Tickets to move AB is not something that you should be doing often, especially early on. It is usually reserved for boosting the AB of later and more difficult maps. Early players should not be Ticketing their AB to maps before Lapis Lakeside.

### Not Immediately Raising Limit

Progress towards the next Sleep Style Dex Goal can be seen in the bubble on the bottom right of the home screen. When you reach a goal, you will have to open it and then press Claim Rewards to receive the rewards, unlock any new maps, and raise the AB limit. Sometimes it may be preferable to not immediately raise your limit, perhaps because you'd rather overflow your AB when your current map is at limit or keep a lower limit to avoid putting more AB into your current map.
 
You are not forced to press Claim Rewards after opening the bubble, so there is no risk of accidentally receiving the rewards. This will not stop progress towards the next goal as it looks at your Sleep Style Dex progress.
